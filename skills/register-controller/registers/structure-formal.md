# Sentence Structure: Formal

Academic register with complex sentence structures, passive voice where appropriate, and no contractions. Suitable for dissertation material and formal literature review notes.

## Core rules

1. **No contractions**: "do not", "it is", "cannot" — always.

2. **Strategic passive voice** (40-60% of sentences): When agent is unimportant, when emphasizing action, or when following disciplinary conventions.
   - Active: "Smith argues that syntactic structure emerges from statistical regularities."
   - Passive: "Forty interviews were conducted following Labovian protocol."

3. **Complex sentence structures**: Multiple clauses per sentence. Average 20-30 words. Embed explanatory material within sentences.
   - "The model employs attention mechanisms, which enable parallel processing and thereby substantially reduce training time compared to sequential architectures."

4. **Formal transitions**: "Moreover", "Furthermore", "Consequently", "Nevertheless", "Specifically", "In this regard", "It should be noted that".

5. **Third person exclusively**: "This analysis suggests...", "The present study demonstrates...", "The findings indicate that..." — no "I" or "you".

## Preferred formal vocabulary

- "demonstrate/reveal/indicate" not "show"
- "employ/implement" not "use"
- "obtain/achieve" not "get"
- "observe/identify" not "find"
- "substantial/significant/notable" not "big/important"
- "examine/investigate/analyze" not "look at"

## Example

**ML (Formal)**: "The transformer architecture represents a fundamental departure from previous sequence transduction models. Rather than processing tokens in sequential order, the model employs multi-head self-attention mechanisms to capture dependencies across all positions simultaneously. When evaluated on the WMT 2014 English-German translation task, the transformer achieves 28.4 BLEU, surpassing the previous state-of-the-art by more than 2 BLEU points while requiring substantially less training time."

## Watch for

- Formal should still be clear — complexity serves precision, not obfuscation
- Do not overuse passive to the point of obscuring agency entirely
- Appropriate for dissertation chapters and formal literature review
